Are my documents uploaded?

No. Photos are captured and assembled into a PDF entirely in your browser with an open-source MIT library (bundled — no CDN). Your documents never leave your device — that is the whole point of this tool.

Why this instead of a scanner app?

Most scanner apps are ad-heavy, add watermarks, or push subscriptions — and many upload your documents to their servers. This one does none of that: free, watermark-free, offline-capable, on-device.

Page order and quality?

Pages appear in the order you capture them; remove any page before downloading. Hold the phone steady and fill the frame with the page for the sharpest result.
