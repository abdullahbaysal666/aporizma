How is the ratio simplified?

The width and height are divided by their greatest common divisor, so 1920×1080 simplifies to 16:9 and 1080×1920 simplifies to 9:16.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No — every calculation happens instantly in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I compute a missing width or height?

Yes — pick a preset ratio (or enter your own), choose which dimension you already know, and the other one is calculated for you.
