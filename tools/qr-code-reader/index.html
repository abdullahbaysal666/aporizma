Is the QR content uploaded anywhere?

No. Frames from your camera and any image you pick are processed entirely in your browser with an open-source MIT library (bundled — no CDN). Nothing leaves your device.

Which codes can it read?

Standard QR codes: links, plain text, Wi-Fi setup codes, e-mail and phone codes. Point the camera steadily; decoding is instant when the code is sharp.

Camera will not open?

Your browser may have denied camera permission. You can still decode from a screenshot or photo using the image option.
